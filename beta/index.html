How it works

Three steps, no visit required

Step 1

They upload a photo & tap their door

The door is the tape measure — a standard door calibrates the whole photo. A satellite roof trace cross-checks the numbers.

Step 2

They draw their lights

Roofline, windows, railings — every run they draw shows live footage and a live price, from your rates.

Step 3

They give details to see it lit up

Name, phone, email, install timing, and whether they own the home — before the AI render generates. You never pay to impress a tire-kicker.

Honest answers

Common questions

Is this a full CRM?

No. It's a quoting and visualisation layer that plugs into what you already use. Your CRM, your scheduling, your invoicing all stay exactly where they are — this handles the part where a homeowner gets a price and you get a qualified lead, then hands off.

How accurate are the measurements?

It's a bidding estimate, not a tape measure. The photo is calibrated off a known object — the front door — and cross-checked against a map trace of the roof. During the beta we tune it using your past jobs, comparing what it produces against what you actually charged, so it's calibrated to your work before it ever faces a customer.

What does it cost after the beta?

The plan is around $2,500 to set up, then a seasonal rate — and beta installers keep a reduced founder rate for life. Nothing is charged during the 2026 season, and you'll see final pricing in writing before you decide whether to carry on.

Do I have to be technical to use it?

No. Setup is done with you — your prices, your packages, your branding, put live on your site. After that it just runs, and quotes land in your inbox. If you can read an email, you can use it.
