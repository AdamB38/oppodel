Honest answers

The questions installers actually ask

How accurate is the measuring?

It calibrates from their front door (a known size) and cross-checks against a satellite trace of their roof. It's a bidding estimate — accurate enough to price confidently, and your crew confirms final footage before install, exactly like you do today. Founding installers test it against their real past jobs before paying anything.

Does the AI render show things the customer didn't buy?

No — every render is checked by a second AI that compares it against exactly what the customer drew. Extra lights, wrong colors, or a wrong house get rejected and re-generated automatically.

How does it get on my website?

We set it up for you — as its own page on your domain or a button on your existing site, with your prices and branding. No tech skills needed on your end.
