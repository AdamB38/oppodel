Privacy

Last updated August 2026

This page covers what happens to the information you give us on this site. It is written to be read, not to be impressive.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form on this site. Depending on the form, that is your name, email address, company name, website, phone number, city or metro, and a few details about your business such as how many jobs you did last season and your average job value.

We do not run advertising or analytics trackers on this site, and we do not collect anything about you beyond what you submit.

What we use it for

To reply to you about the thing you enquired about, and to send you information about the product launch or beta you signed up for. That is the whole purpose.

We do not sell your information, rent it, or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Where it goes

Form submissions are sent to our own lead system and stored with our website host so a submission is not lost if one of them is unavailable. Both are used only to deliver the enquiry to us.

  • Netlify hosts this website and stores form submissions
  • Cloudflare runs the endpoint that receives submissions

How long we keep it

For as long as we are in contact with you about your enquiry, and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you come back to us. If you ask us to delete it, we delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Every email we send has an unsubscribe option, and replying to ask us to stop works just as well.

Contact

Email hello@quotal.org for anything on this page, including deletion requests.
